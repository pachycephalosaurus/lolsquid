The “tempo ratio” of a canon in two is the ratio between the tempos of the two voices (for example, a canon with a tempo ratio of 2 has a second voice being performed at twice the speed of the first)

This audio file (called Canon C) is a snippet of a canon in two, where each voice is its own canon (so technically a canon in four). Let the first voice be Canon A and the second voice be Canon B. Canon A has a tempo ratio of α, and Canon B has a tempo ratio of β. This means that the total tempo ratio of Canon C (the audio file) is β/α = γ. Calculate γ to 5 decimal places.
