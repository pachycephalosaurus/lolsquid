Goodness gracious. I think the best first step is to determine what this event is, which should give us insight as to whether this is actually Xi Mingze (it is not). Google RIS on the F4D logo returns Fashion4Development, an NGO developed "in cooperation with the office of the United Nations to support the UN goals" (whatever those may be). The specific event is the annual First Ladies Luncheon, "a ceremony held during the UN General Assembly week in New York that celebrates the unprecedented cooperation between diplomacy and fashion for the greater good of women and children worldwide." :-) how nice!

2024 was F4D's 12th annual Luncheon, so we're going to have to try and find which specific year this is from. This shouldn't be too difficult, as each backdrop looks slightly different from year to year. If we notice the Pierre Hotel logo on the backdrop, a Google search tells us that the 7th and 8th Luncheons were hosted at the Pierre Hotel (it is possible that other ones were hosted here, but the 8th Annual Luncheon is the only one with a backdrop matching that in the image). Now we just have to flip through photos from the event until we find our person of interest, and check its source. Shutterstock Editorial has what we're looking for here, showing that this is in fact Fi (Catherine) Chen, seen with Noelle Xie (and NOT Peng Liyuan!)

A possible point of confusion is that Michaela Zee was fancast as Xi Mingye in "Falong Gong In The Century", so some Google results may return her as a possible candidate. Looking at what Michaele Zee actually looks like, though, it is clear that she is not the individual in the photo.
